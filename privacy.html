Privacy policy

Raat Ki Kahani publishes its own videos to its own Instagram and YouTube accounts using the official APIs. The app only uploads and publishes content created by Raat Ki Kahani. It does not collect, store or share personal data of viewers or other users.

Google user data: the YouTube Data API is used only to upload videos to the Raat Ki Kahani channel. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted as GitHub Actions secrets and are never shared with third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
